Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ClipSnap is committed to user privacy. We do not require users to create an account, register, or provide personal credentials. We do not log, track, or save the URLs you submit or the media files you download.

2. Server Logs and Technical Data

Like standard web services, our servers may temporarily record generic web requests (such as browser user-agent and IP addresses) strictly for diagnostic, security, and traffic load distribution purposes. This technical data is never linked to individual identity.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

We may partner with third-party advertising networks to display ads. These vendors, including third-party ad networks, may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting standard network advertising opt-out pages.

4. External Links

Our website processes public URLs from third-party social platforms (Facebook, Instagram, X). We have no control over the privacy practices or content policies of those third-party platforms.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through our Contact Page.
