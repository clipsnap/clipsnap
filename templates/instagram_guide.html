Step 1. Copy the Instagram video link

Copy the link on your phone

Open the Instagram app and find the video or Reel. Tap the paper plane (Share) icon under the post, then tap Copy link. The link is now on your clipboard, and Instagram usually confirms it with a short message. This method works the same way on iPhone and Android.

Copy the link on a computer

Go to instagram.com and click the video so it opens on its own page. Then click the address bar, select the whole URL, and copy it with Ctrl+C on Windows or Cmd+C on a Mac. If the video only plays inside your feed, click its timestamp first to open the dedicated post page.

Check that it is a post link

A downloader needs the link to one specific post, not to a profile. Use this table to check yours.

The link must point to a single public post, or the downloader has nothing to fetch.
